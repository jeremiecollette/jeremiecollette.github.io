New Chess Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

This policy explains what information New Chess - Escape Puzzles ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is made by Jeremie Collette ("I", "me").

In short: you don't need an account, and I never ask for your name, email or location. Your progress stays on your device. Anonymous gameplay statistics are only collected if you agree. Ads in the game are provided by Unity, and they are only personalised if you allow it.

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, coins, unlocked packs, purchases (such as No Ads) and settings are saved on your device only. I don't receive this information. Deleting the game deletes it.

Gameplay statistics (only if you agree)

When you first open the game, it asks whether you want to share anonymous gameplay data. If you say yes, the game uses Unity Analytics (by Unity Technologies) to send information such as:

  • levels started, completed, failed or skipped, and how long they took;
  • level packs unlocked, and coins earned and spent;
  • settings changes, ads watched and in-app purchases made (which item, not payment details);
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language), and a random ID that Unity creates for this installation. Unity may also work out your approximate country from your IP address.

I use this only to understand how the game is played and to improve it, for example to find levels that are too hard. It isn't used to identify you.

Your choice: you can turn this on or off at any time in Settings → Data Sharing. To delete the data already collected, hold "Hold to delete my data" in Settings. This asks Unity to delete it and turns sharing off.

Ads

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay and the Unity Ads network. These are an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and ads you choose to watch for coins. To show and measure ads, and to prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • your device's advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking when iOS asks;
  • an app-specific device identifier, your IP address and basic device information;
  • how you interact with ads (for example whether an ad was shown, watched or tapped).

Ads are personalised to your interests only if you agreed to data sharing and allowed tracking in the iOS prompt. Otherwise you still see ads, but they aren't based on your activity in other apps. You can change your tracking choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Buying No Ads removes the ads between levels. Ads you choose to watch for coins stay available.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. I never see or receive your payment details. The game receives only a confirmation of what was bought, so it can give you your coins or remove ads.

Who receives information

I don't sell your personal information. Information is shared only with the services described above, which process it under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity Technologies (Unity Analytics, Unity LevelPlay, Unity Ads): Unity Privacy Policy and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy
  • Apple (App Store purchases): Apple Privacy Policy

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has shared information through the game, contact me and I will have it deleted.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, and to withdraw your consent. You can withdraw consent and delete analytics data directly in the game's Settings. For anything else, contact me at the address below.
