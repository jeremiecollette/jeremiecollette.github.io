New Chess Support

Stuck on a level, found a bug, or have an idea? Email me at newchessescapepuzzles@gmail.com and I'll get back to you as soon as I can. Telling me your device and the level number helps.

Common questions

I bought No Ads on another device, or reinstalled the game.
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases.

A purchase didn't give me my coins.
Close and reopen the game: unfinished purchases are delivered at launch. If they still don't appear, email me.

There's no sound.
Check the Sound and Music settings in the game's Settings, and your device volume.

How do I delete my data?
In Settings, hold Hold to delete my data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
